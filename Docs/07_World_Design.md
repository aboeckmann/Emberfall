Last Updated: 2026-07-04

## World Structure

The world consists of two connected components.

### Personal Bastion
Private progression space. Full detail: `08_Bastion_System.md`.

### Adventure World
Shared regional world. Players travel between regions rather than freely exploring an open world.

Regions contain:

- Towns
- Dungeons
- Bosses
- Hidden discoveries
- Guild halls
- Quests
